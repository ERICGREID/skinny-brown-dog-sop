Skinny Brown Dog Media

Publishing System SOP Flipbook

A comprehensive operations guide for scalable, high-quality author support.

Introduction & Overview

Welcome to the Skinny Brown Dog Media Publishing System SOP. This guide outlines our full workflow and quality systems for scalable indie publishing.

Author Onboarding Process

Includes intake form, publishing agreement, tax forms, and marketing questionnaire.

BookVault Setup & Management

Grant correct access, complete metadata, and guide authors through Stripe Connect setup.

IngramSpark Setup & Management

Configure titles, disable expanded distribution, enable global reach. Check discounts.

KDP Setup & Management

Guide metadata setup, pricing, promo scheduling, and upload process.

File Preparation & Quality Control

Use QA checklist before upload. Ensure correct trim, DPI, bleed, and embedded fonts.

Author Communication Protocols

Weekly updates, milestone confirmations, and use of templates. All comms saved in Drive.

Payment Systems & Management

Direct author payments via Stripe/PayPal. Use payout tracker to resolve issues.

Marketing Support Services

Plan using author responses, Promo Planner spreadsheet, and templated outreach.

Troubleshooting Common Issues

File errors, payout delays, and listing issues. Follow escalation protocol and document steps.

Author Exit Process

Use exit checklist. Archive files, deliver final assets, and issue rights reversion letter.

System Maintenance & Auditing

Run quarterly audits with checklist. Validate tool access, templates, and author experience.

Resources & Support Contacts

Email templates, intake form, proof form, file checklist, promo planner, and contacts for BookVault, IngramSpark, KDP.
